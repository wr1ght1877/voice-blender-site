How it works

  1. 1

    Start a call

    Open one call and just talk, like you would to a teammate.

  2. 2

    Summon your bots

    Bring in the bots you need. Each one joins on its own colored line.

  3. 3

    They take turns

    No talking over each other. Every answer is labeled with who said it, and you stay in charge.
