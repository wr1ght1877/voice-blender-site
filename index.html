FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Voice Blender?

A way for several AI bots to share one live voice call. You call a bot by name, it answers in its own voice, and a turn-taking mixer makes sure the bots don’t talk over each other or over you.

How does a bot know I’m talking to it?

Wake words. A listener picks up bot names in what you say, like “Hey Planner”, “Researcher” or “Builder”, and hands the question that follows to that bot.

Won’t the bots talk over each other?

That’s what the turn-taking mixer is for. It queues bot speech so only one voice talks at a time, and uses cues like silence length, falling pitch and energy drops to tell when a speaker has finished.

Can I interrupt a bot?

Yes. When you start talking, bot audio stops or ducks. You stay in charge of the call.

How many bots can join one call?

It depends on the plan: one bot on Yap (plus a 2-bot taste for 10 minutes a month, so you can hear the blender), up to three on Chorus, and up to five of your own bots on Symphony.

How do minutes work?

Each plan includes call minutes every month: 30 on Yap (up to 10 a day), 300 on Chorus and 400 on Symphony. Minutes reset monthly and don’t roll over. You’ll get a heads-up at 80%. When you hit the limit, the call stops, unless you’ve turned on extra minutes (10¢/min on Chorus, 15¢/min on Symphony) and set your own spending limit. No surprise bills. Yap’s 10 minutes of 2-bot calls count inside its 30 free minutes. Yap has no extra minutes; upgrade to keep talking.

Can I save by paying ahead?

Yes. Chorus is $24 a month, or $69 for 3 months, $129 for 6 months or $240 for a year ($20 a month). Symphony is $59 a month, or $168 for 3 months, $318 for 6 months or $590 for a year (about $49 a month). That’s up to 17% off.

What can I do with it?

Create and do anything with your own personal bots, together: have a meeting with all your bots at once, brainstorm ideas between them, or set up a team of bots and build your very own project together. You stay in control of your team with one voice.
